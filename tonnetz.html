Reading the map

  • → / ←  perfect fifth (P5) / fourth (P4) — the circle of fifths
  • ↗ / ↙  major third (M3) / minor sixth (m6)
  • ↘ / ↖  minor third (m3) / major sixth (M6)
  • upward triangles are major triads, downward are minor; diminished & augmented triads are straight lines along the m3 / M3 axes
